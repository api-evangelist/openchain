Asyntec SpA, a technology and consulting services company based in Santiago, Chile, has announced an OpenChain ISO/IEC 5230:2020 conformant program and joined the OpenChain Community of Conformance. ISO/IEC 5230:2020 is the international standard for open source license compliance. It defines the key requirements for a high-quality open source compliance program, helping organizations manage license obligations, ensure component transparency, and mitigate risk across the software supply chain.
