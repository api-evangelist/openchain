Vaultr has self-certified conformance with ISO/IEC 5230:2020, the international standard for open source license compliance. Vaultr is a local-first AI platform built specifically for legal teams, centered around Lex—an on-device assistant designed to read, draft, and reason across legal matters without data ever leaving the firm’s infrastructure. By completing OpenChain ISO/IEC 5230 self-certification, Vaultr reinforces its commitment to transparency, trust, and rigor across both its data privacy architecture and its open source supply chain.
