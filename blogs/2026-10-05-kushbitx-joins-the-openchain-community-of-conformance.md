KushBitx has self-certified conformance to OpenChain ISO/IEC 5230:2020 and ISO/IEC 18974:2023 for a narrowly defined supplied-open-source software program. The scope covers its publicly distributed @kushbitx/sdk and directly related open-source components supplied with SpendGuard/AgentProof releases. Internal-only systems and third-party services consumed by KushBitx are outside that scope.
